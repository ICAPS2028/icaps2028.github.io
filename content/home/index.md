The [International Conference on Automated Planning and Scheduling](https://www.icaps-conference.org/) (ICAPS) is the premier forum for exchanging news and research results on the theory and applications of intelligent and automated planning and scheduling technology.

Planning and scheduling involve the computational selection and sequencing of actions to achieve goals and optimize objectives. These technologies support intelligent decision-making in applications including industry, aerospace, supply chains, robotics, software engineering, education, and digital entertainment.

**ICAPS 2028** is the 38th edition of the ICAPS conference series and will take place in **Naples, Italy, in July 2028**. We are organizing the conference on the **Naples waterfront**. Exact conference dates and submission deadlines will be announced.
